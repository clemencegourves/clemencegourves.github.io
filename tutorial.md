Hands-on session presented at the Lorentz Center workshop
*Star-Planet Interactions: Entangled Fates and Open Futures* (November 2026).

A close-in companion does not need to transit to leave a mark on its star's
light curve: it can induce a small, periodic brightness modulation at its
orbital period. In this tutorial you search for such a signal in the PSD of
*Kepler* light curves, and learn to tell it apart from stellar activity.

**What you will learn**

1. Load *Kepler* long-cadence light curves and stellar properties
2. Compute the PSD of a light curve
3. Tell a stellar-activity peak apart from a candidate orbital signal
4. Measure the period and check its stability by phase folding
